# 🚀 Dayflow HRMS

> **Every workday, perfectly aligned.** Dayflow is a modern, unified Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed to seamlessly manage team scheduling, attendance, leave approvals, payroll statements, and AI-driven workplace analytics under a single, intuitive interface.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://nanmozhi-25.github.io/dayflow_hrms/](https://nanmozhi-25.github.io/dayflow_hrms/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Employee & Manager Portals
* **Role-Based Views:** Easily switch between Employee and Manager/Admin perspectives.
* **Profile Management:** Comprehensive view of personal details, employment contracts, salary structures, and downloadable professional verification documents (Resume, National ID, Offer Contract).

### ⏰ Attendance & Time-Off Management
* **Clock Punch Actions:** Flexible office and remote check-in/check-out tracking.
* **Attendance Statistics:** Real-time tracking of attendance rates, total productive hours, and shift status.
* **Leave Requests:** Intuitive leave application system supporting Annual, Sick, and Casual leaves with real-time balance bars and status tracking history.

### 💰 Payroll & Compensation
* **Monthly Statements:** Detailed breakdown of earnings (Basic salary, allowances) and deductions (Taxes, health insurance, provident fund).
* **Payment Tracking:** Secure direct deposit logs, banking institution details, masked account numbers, and downloadable monthly payslip PDFs.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Workplace Insights
* **Predictive Analytics:** Machine learning models evaluate check-in habits and output rates to forecast workplace trends.
* **Fatigue & Concurrency Alerts:** Proactive warnings for overtime burnout risks and overlapping leave requests with automated recommendations.

### 🔔 Notifications & Settings
* **System Feed:** Centralized notification log for approved requests, payroll releases, and urgent action required items.
* **Customization:** Manage email preferences and update security credentials seamlessly.
